**Mission Domain Name:** General Purpose, Multi-Role & Custom Missions

## Description

UAV missions focused on flexible, configurable, multi-role, customer-specific, experimental, and emerging applications that do not belong exclusively to a single mission domain or combine multiple operational objectives.

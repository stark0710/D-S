# Assembly Instructions for FixedWing_SURVEY_Production

## Sequence Steps:
1. Mount flight controller and avionics onto battery tray in fuselage core.
2. Screw brushless motor firewall onto front fuselage bulkhead.
3. Slide left and right wing panels together using central carbon rod spar sleeve joints.
4. Torque wing mounts down onto fuselage floor plates using nylon M5 bolts.
5. Hook elevator and rudder control horns up to tail servos.

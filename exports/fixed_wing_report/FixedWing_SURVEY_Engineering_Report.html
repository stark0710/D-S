    A.1 Lift Equation Reference

    The lift force at stall is given by:

    L = 0.5 × ρ × V² × S × CL_max

    Where:

  • ρ = air density (kg/m³)
  • V = airspeed (m/s)
  • S = reference wing area (m²)
  • CL_max = maximum lift coefficient

    • A.2 Drag Polar

    CD = CD0 + K × CL²

    Where K = 1 / (π × AR × e) is the induced drag factor.

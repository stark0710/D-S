# Torq Wings VTOL — Phase 12 Controlled Flight Testing & Flight-Envelope Expansion Report
**Date:** 2026-09-21 | **Aircraft:** Torq Wings VTOL (Lift + Cruise QuadPlane) | **Campaign:** Torq Wings Phase 12 Controlled Flight Test Campaign

## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 12 conducts the first controlled flight-test campaign for the Torq Wings Lift + Cruise VTOL, progressing incrementally from initial low-risk VTOL lift through sustained hover, forward acceleration, wing-borne transition, fixed-wing cruise, return transition, and vertical touchdown. **Campaign Verdict: FLIGHT_ENVELOPE_PARTIALLY_DEMONSTRATED**

## 2. Scope

This document establishes the empirical, evidence-based flight envelope of the aircraft. Per strict safety rules (Section 2 & 44), a successful single flight does NOT represent full validation. All data herein represents demonstrated flight measurements rather than certified boundaries.

## 3. Safety Framework

- Go/Abort/Emergency conditions established prior to every flight sortie.
- Sub-second transmitter emergency kill switch (CH7) and physical XT90-S isolation.
- Automatic return-to-launch (RTL) at 40m AGL upon telemetry loss (>30s) or RC failsafe.
- Single-variable incremental expansion rule strictly enforced between sorties.
